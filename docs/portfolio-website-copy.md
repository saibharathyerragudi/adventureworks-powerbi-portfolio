## Case Study Copy

Problem: AdventureWorks needed a single dashboard to monitor revenue, profit, orders, returns, customer behavior, product performance, and regional sales performance.

Solution: Built an interactive Power BI report with executive KPI cards, revenue trends, product drillthrough, customer segmentation, geographic sales analysis, dynamic metric selectors, slicers, bookmarks, buttons, and tooltip interactions.

Impact: The report gives business users a path from executive summary to detailed product, customer, and territory analysis in one cohesive analytics experience.
